San Pietro Church

Corniglia · Cinque Terre e Golfo dei Poeti

Located in the upper part of the village, the Church of San Pietro is one of the most fascinating examples of Ligurian Gothic architecture in the region. Built in 1334 on the ruins of an ancient 11th-century building, the church greets you with a facade of local sandstone that glows like gold at sunset. The highlight of the exterior is the magnificent white Carrara marble rose window: a masterpiece of perforated stone so delicate it looks like lace, contrasting beautifully with the sturdy medieval walls.

Stepping inside, the atmosphere shifts: the interior is sober and elegant, with Baroque influences blending seamlessly with the original Gothic lines. Be sure to look down at the 12th-century baptismal font and look up at the frescoed vaults. A fascinating detail for visitors is its location: the church was built right on the ridge of the hill, and its courtyard serves as a natural terrace. From here, the silence of the village and the view stretching toward the valley offer a different, more spiritual perspective of the Cinque Terre, away from the typical seaside hustle.
