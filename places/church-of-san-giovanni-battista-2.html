Church of San Giovanni Battista

Riomaggiore · Cinque Terre e Golfo dei Poeti

Welcome to the Church of San Giovanni Battista, Riomaggiore’s most significant religious landmark and the keeper of its historical memory. Built in 1340, this church stands in the upper part of the village, offering an atmosphere of rare serenity away from the lively bustle of the harbor.

Take a moment to admire the façade: although it was renovated in Neogothic style during the 19th century, it still features its most precious original element—the white Carrara marble rose window, a 14th-century stone masterpiece that glows under the Ligurian sun. Step inside and you’ll find an unexpected Baroque elegance, with aisles housing treasures such as the wooden crucifix by Anton Maria Maragliano.

The church is easily reached by walking up the main street (Via Colombo). It is the ideal starting point before continuing your climb to the Riomaggiore Castle, located just a few steps away. Visiting this church means immersing yourself in the authentic soul of the Cinque Terre, amidst art, faith and timeless views.
