Storia e curiosità

The Village Reached by 377 Steps

Corniglia is the only Cinque Terre village set high above the sea rather than directly on the water. From the railway station, the famous brick staircase called the Lardarina climbs to the village in 377 steps.

The Terrace Left by a Church Lost to the Sea

Corniglia's Santa Maria viewpoint preserves the memory of a small church that once stood near the cliff edge and was lost in the mid-19th century. The open terrace now offers a broad view over the Ligurian Sea.
