Montale's Yellow Pagoda by the Sea

Eugenio Montale spent many summers in the family villa at Fegina, nicknamed the "yellowish pagoda". Monterosso's sea, lemons and dry-stone landscape became recurring images in the Nobel laureate's poetry.
