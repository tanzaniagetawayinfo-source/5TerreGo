Manarola Scenic Viewpoint

Manarola · Cinque Terre e Golfo dei Poeti

To reach this spot, simply follow the coastal path that starts from Manarola harbor and winds along the cliffside to the north (towards Corniglia). After a short walk along Punta Bonfiglio, you will be standing directly in front of the most colorful facade of the village.

The Magical Moment at Sunset…

While it is stunning at any time of day, the real magic happens at sunset. As the sun dips below the skyline, the pastel houses light up with golden and orange hues and the village lights begin to twinkle like a small nativity scene over the water.
