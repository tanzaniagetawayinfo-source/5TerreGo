"Via Dell' Amore"

Via dell’Amore is the most famous walking path in the Cinque Terre, connecting Riomaggiore and Manarola along a dramatic stretch of coastline. Carved into the cliffs above the Ligurian Sea, the path is known for its romantic atmosphere, easy walk, and panoramic views. It is short but extremely scenic, making it one of the most iconic experiences in the Cinque Terre.
