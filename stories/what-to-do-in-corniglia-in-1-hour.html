What to Do in Corniglia in One Hour: A Quick but Unmissable Itinerary If you only have sixty minutes to explore Corniglia, don’t worry — this charming hilltop village in the Cinque Terre is compact enough for a relaxed and rewarding visit. Once you arrive at the train station (or after climbing the famous Lardarina staircase with its 377 steps), the village unfolds easily on foot. Here’s the perfect one-hour itinerary: Start by wandering through the narrow, colorful carrugi (typical Ligurian alleys) of the historic center. A short stroll will lead you to Largo Taragio, the main square, home to the pretty Oratorio di Santa Caterina. From there, continue to the Church of San Pietro, one of the most beautiful religious buildings in the Cinque Terre. Built in the 14th century in Gothic-Ligurian style, it’s worth a quick visit for its elegant façade and richly detailed interior. The real highlight, however, is the Santa Maria Terrace (also known as the Belvedere), located at the end of Via Fieschi. This panoramic viewpoint offers a breathtaking 180° vista over the neighboring villages, the sparkling sea, and the iconic terraced vineyards. It’s the most memorable moment of your stop in Corniglia. In short, the must-see spots in one hour are:

  • A walk through the historic alleys and Largo Taragio
  • The Church of San Pietro
  • The Santa Maria panoramic terrace

At a comfortable pace, you can comfortably enjoy all of this without rushing — taking photos and soaking up the authentic, peaceful atmosphere of what many consider the most genuine village in the Cinque Terre. If you have a few extra minutes, treat yourself to an artisanal gelato or a coffee on the terrace before heading back down to the station. Corniglia proves that even with limited time, you can still experience something truly special and beautiful.
