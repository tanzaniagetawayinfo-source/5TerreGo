What to Do When Cinque Terre Trails Are Closed

A trail closure does not cancel the trip: use a safe plan built around villages, rail, culture and nearby towns.

3 min read · 16 July 2026 · 5TerreGo Editorial

Do not improvise around a closure

Closures can reflect landslides, maintenance, weather alerts or crowd management. A barrier is not an invitation to find a social-media shortcut. Check the National Park’s live map and ask an information point whether a suitable official alternative exists for your experience and footwear.

Build a village day

Use the train to pair contrasting places: Riomaggiore with Corniglia, or Vernazza with Monterosso. Slow down for churches, small harbours, terraces and local food. A boat can add a coastal perspective when services run and sea conditions allow, but it is not a guaranteed substitute in bad weather.

Look beyond the five names

La Spezia offers museums, markets and a waterfront; Levanto adds a beach and a lived-in town centre. On a clear day, higher settlements and sanctuaries may be possibilities only if their official paths are open and appropriate. During adverse weather, stay off the trail network entirely.

A flexible itinerary is a safety tool. Save one indoor plan and one low-exertion plan before arrival, then make the final choice using same-day information.

Plan with live information. Check Cinque Terre National Park for trail access and alerts, and Trenitalia for current rail conditions and fares.
