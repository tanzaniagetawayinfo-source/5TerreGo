Ten Mistakes First-Time Visitors Make in Cinque Terre

The avoidable errors that create stress, from overpacking the itinerary to treating live trail conditions as optional.

3 min read · 16 July 2026 · 5TerreGo Editorial

Planning mistakes

  1. Trying to visit all five villages between breakfast and lunch.
  2. Booking a steep apartment without asking about steps.
  3. Assuming every famous trail is open.
  4. Using an old timetable screenshot instead of official live information.
  5. Buying a travel card without comparing it with actual journeys.

Behaviour mistakes

  1. Wearing sandals or smooth soles on hiking paths.
  2. Blocking station stairs and residential doorways for photographs.
  3. Swimming from rocks without reading sea conditions.
  4. Leaving dinner and the return train to chance in peak season.
  5. Treating the villages as scenery rather than communities.

The better approach

Choose fewer places, stay longer and check current conditions each morning. Build one weather-proof alternative and keep enough time to miss a train without losing the day. Spend money with local businesses, take your rubbish away and accept a closure without searching for a forbidden shortcut.

Cinque Terre is small on a map but physically intense. The trip becomes easier when the plan respects gradients, crowds, weather and ordinary local life.

Plan with live information. Check Cinque Terre National Park for trail access and alerts, and Trenitalia for current rail conditions and fares.
