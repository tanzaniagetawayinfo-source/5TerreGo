How to Use Cinque Terre Trains Without Losing Your Day

Platforms, ticket rules, peak-hour strategy and the small habits that make village-hopping easier.

3 min read · 16 July 2026 · 5TerreGo Editorial

Think in direction, not just destination

The five villages sit on the same coastal railway between La Spezia and Levanto. Before entering the station, know whether you are travelling north or south and read the platform display for the train number and final destination. Some fast trains pass through without stopping, so never board from memory alone.

Buy the right product

In the main 2026 visitor season, Trenitalia applies dedicated Cinque Terre fares with A, B and C price bands. A single ticket is for one journey, while the Cinque Terre Train MS Card covers unlimited regional travel between La Spezia and Levanto plus listed Park services. Compare the current cost with the number of journeys you genuinely expect to make.

Validate paper tickets when required. Digital products and cards can follow different rules, so read the conditions shown with the product you purchased. Keep your ticket or named card and identification available for inspection.

Avoid the platform crush

Mid-morning arrivals and late-afternoon returns are often the busiest. Leave earlier, stay for dinner or move in the opposite direction to the largest flows. Stand behind safety markings, let passengers exit first and avoid blocking narrow underpasses while checking your phone.

Official schedules can change. Use Trenitalia’s current journey planner and station displays on the day, not screenshots saved weeks earlier.

Plan with live information. Check Cinque Terre National Park for trail access and alerts, and Trenitalia for current rail conditions and fares.
