Train vs Boat in the Cinque Terre

Wich is better? Pros and cons

4 min read· 30 April 2026· 5TerreGo

The two primary ways to explore the 5 terre coastline are by train and by boat. Both options offer completely different experiences. Let’s take a look together at the advantages and disadvantages of both in order to help you choose the best one.

Visiting by Boat Exploring by boat offers a spectacular and exclusive view, whether you use the public ferry or opt for a private tour.

Pros

  • Breathtaking panoramic views: You can admire the famous colorful houses perched on the cliffs exactly as they appear on postcards—a view impossible to get from the train.
  • Relaxing experience: Traveling by sea allows you to enjoy the sun, the sea breeze, and the slow pace of the coast, far away from the chaos of the train platforms.
  • Private tour are flexible: Choosing a private boat excursion allows you to do a lot of activities, such as snorkelling and swimming next to hidden coves usually inaccessible from land.

Cons

  • The Weather: Ferries and boats do not sail in rough seas or strong winds for safety rules . Cancellations are frequent especially during no summer seasons
  • Higher prices: A daily ferry ticket is significantly more expensive than the train, and the price goes up considerably if you book a private tour or boat rental.
  • No stops in Corniglia: Public ferries cannot dock at Corniglia. If you only choose the boat, you will have to skip this village or combine your trip with another mode of transport.
  • Longer travel times: Moving by water is much slower than trains…

Visiting by Train The train is the most common, fast, and budget-friendly way to visit 5 terre. The local railway line cuts right through the coastal rock, connecting all the villages in just a few minutes.

Pros

  • Frequent and fast: The Cinque Terre Express trains run every 15–20 minutes during high season and take only 2–5 minutes to travel between stations.
  • Access to Corniglia: The train allows you to reach Corniglia, the only village perched high on the cliffs without a harbor for ferry docking.
  • It’s cheaper : there are many passes(like the Cinque Terre Card) that offer unlimited rides at affordable rates, which also includes bus transfers and access to the hiking trails.
  • Weather-independent: The train service operates regularly even during rough seas or rainy days.

Cons

  • stress for crowds: Especially During summer season and weekends.
  • No views during the ride: The railway line runs almost entirely through underground tunnels. You will not see the sea while moving from one village to the next and you will not see the panoramic view that usually appears on postcards.
  • Stairs and steep inclines: Many stations require walking up and down sets of stairs, which is a major factor to consider if you are traveling with heavy luggages.

So, which is the best way?

In my opinion, you could Choose the train if You are short on time and if you want to save money. Moreover, you will be sure of visiting all the 5terre (including Corniglia). On the other side, I think you should opt for the boat if you prefer a romantic and relaxing experience. You will also take iconic photos avoiding crowded train stations.

Let me give you an insider Tip: use both ! For example,if you have one day only,Buy a one-way train ticket to move quickly between villages in the morning…take the ferry in the late afternoon to enjoy a relaxing ride when travelling back to your starting area instead!
