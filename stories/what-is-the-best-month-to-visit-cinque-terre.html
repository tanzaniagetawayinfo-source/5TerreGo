What is the best month to visit Cinque Terre?

One of the biggest dilemmas for travelers

3 min read· 26 May 2026· 5TerreGo

One of the is biggest dilemma for travelers

Everybody ask me wich is the best month to plan their trip to Cinque Terre. For sure it s an hard question especially because each traveler has different priorities from another … that’s why I think the best way to find the answer is to analyse pregi e difetti of each period of the year !

June to September

Advantages

  • the sea is warm and you can swim in it also by night
  • all restaurants,trails and accommodations are opened
  • the days are long so you’ll have more time for exploring
  • the weather is almost sunny

Disadvantages

  • Crowded . A lot. Everywhere: trails, restaurants,viewpoints
  • Highest prices
  • the trains are often delayed (the crowds need much time for getting in and off )

The crowds are definitely the biggest disadvantage , so this period is perfect for someone who wants to explore the places for the whole day without hurry and who doesn’t care about crowds

November to March

Advantages

  • few tourists
  • Lower prices
  • from December to January, in Manarola, you can admire the famous Christmas Crib that is located on a hill near the village
  • Relax, silence, authenticity

Disadvantages

  • weather is very changeable and it can pour down very often (even floods happen here),
  • the days are shorter
  • many restaurants, accommodations and trails(for safety rules) are closed
  • Less trains and ferries
  • It’s very very windy and cold especially from December to February

This period is perfect if you want to live in a surreal atmosphere made by silence and rough waves…

April,May,October

Advantages

  • if the weather is nice you can already/still swim in the sea,
  • There are many tourists but no crowds yet
  • trails are opened and well prepared, as the majority of restaurants and accommodations
  • the prices in restaurants and accommodations are still lower than in high seaso
  • the weather is perfect, but…

Disadvantages

  • …still very changeable, you can find warm and sunny days as well as cold and rainy days
  • when after the winter season the trails are damaged, many of them can be still closed for the tourists … even until the end of April.

Honestly ? Maybe This is the best period for coming…but it needs also to be a little bit lucky !

So now tell me…Wich one would you choose?
