Monterosso Old Town and Beach: A Balanced Day

A full-day plan combining the broadest beach in Cinque Terre with history, food and the older settlement.

3 min read · 16 July 2026 · 5TerreGo Editorial

Start by the sea

Use the cooler morning for a walk along Fegina and the beach. Identify free and managed sections before choosing a place, follow local swimming guidance and keep bags protected. The promenade is one of the easiest places in the five villages for a level walk.

Cross into the old town

The tunnel links the newer beach area with the historic centre. Explore the striped façade of San Giovanni Battista, small lanes and food shops, then take lunch away from the station rush. Monterosso’s scale rewards a full day more than a quick platform stop.

Choose one afternoon

Return to the water, climb toward the convent viewpoints, or begin a verified open trail only with enough daylight, water and suitable footwear. The path toward Vernazza is strenuous and stepped; its popularity does not make it an easy promenade.

Stay for evening if your schedule allows. The beach empties, the old town changes pace and later rail travel can be calmer than the main afternoon return.

Plan with live information. Check Cinque Terre National Park for trail access and alerts, and Trenitalia for current rail conditions and fares.
